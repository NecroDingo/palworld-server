Current Mod List:

🎁 **Daily Login Rewards**<br>
Rewards you for logging in regularly with a 30-day streak system — the longer you keep the streak alive, the better the payout gets. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/2164

🌟 **Expedition XP - Pals Level While Away**<br>
Pals sent on expeditions now earn real levels/XP on top of loot, using the game's normal leveling curve. Great way to level up under-leveled Pals without deploying them. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4315

📈 **Pals Learn Over Time**<br>
Reworks how Pal work-skill leveling is priced so a Pal's natural stats actually matter — a Pal with high base skills levels up cheaply, while a low-base Pal has a much steeper (but doable) climb. Makes catching a genuinely good Pal worth it instead of just grinding whatever you tamed first. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4316

:soccer: **Recover Pal Spheres**<br>
Gives you back Pal Spheres that miss instead of letting them vanish — misses land nearby as pickups (or drop straight into your inventory if unreachable), and it works with hand throws and all the sphere launchers. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4547

:moneybag: **Guild Merchant**<br>
Combines the inventories of every merchant your guild has captured into one shared shop — deploy any captured merchant and it offers everything the guild's collected, so no more running between bases to find one item. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4863

📚 **Schematic Shelf - Bookshelves Hold Blueprints**<br>
Turns the Antique Bookshelf into a dedicated 200-slot schematic library. Only blueprints go in — junk items get rejected — and it doesn't touch any other storage (chests, coolers, fridges stay vanilla). It's all handled on the server. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4314

📦 **Base Camp Item Stacker**<br>
Auto-stacks items dropped on the ground in your base if a matching item is already nearby, so your base doesn't turn into a field of hundreds of individual item drops. Also helps server performance since fewer stacks are floating around. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/1999

📏 **AntiPhat - Pal Resize**<br>
Shrinks oversized Pals (looking at you, Mammorest) down to something reasonable so they don't block your whole base. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/3799

I know there isn't PVP at the moment, but found this and put it in just incase we decide to PVP

🛡️ **Offline Raid Protection (PVP)**<br>
Protects your guild's base from damage whenever every member of the guild is offline, and turns vulnerability back on the moment anyone logs in. Keeps PVP raiding fair instead of punishing people for having jobs/sleep schedules. - https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/2224
